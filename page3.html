A creative and technical perspective

Where Music Meets Data Architecture

Music is a creative field, but technology is involved in almost every part of modern music. Artists use software to record and edit songs, platforms use data to organize music libraries, and listeners interact with recommendation systems every day.

How Data Appears in Music

Streaming platforms work with information about songs, artists, releases, playlists, and listening activity. Organized data can help companies understand how music is discovered and how audiences interact with different releases.

Why Data Architecture Matters

Data architecture provides a structure for how information is collected, stored, connected, protected, and used. A data architect can help organizations design systems that allow information to be reliable, organized, and useful.

Technology in Music Production

Digital audio workstations, microphones, plugins, mixing tools, and distribution platforms have changed how artists create music. An artist can record an idea, edit it, add effects, and prepare it for release using digital tools.

My Career Connection

I want to learn both the creative and technical sides of my interests. Data architecture can give me a path into technology, while music allows me to continue developing creatively.

My Goals

  • Complete my Business and Information Technology Management degree.
  • Develop stronger database and programming skills.
  • Learn more about data architecture and data systems.
  • Continue developing as a musician.
  • Explore technology used in the music industry.
  • Find ways to combine data and creativity in my future career.

Conclusion

Music and data architecture are two different interests that can still connect through technology. Both require organization, attention to detail, and an ability to recognize patterns.
