Music and Data Architecture

Welcome to my personal website. My interests combine music, creativity, business, and technology. I am currently studying Business and Information Technology Management and I am interested in building a future career in data architecture while continuing to develop as a musician.

My Interest in Music

Music is an important creative outlet for me. Writing, recording, producing, and learning how music is made all require creativity and attention to detail. I enjoy the process of turning an idea into something that other people can hear.

My Interest in Data

Data architecture interests me because organizations depend on information to make decisions. Data architecture involves planning how information is organized, stored, connected, and made available to the people and systems that need it.

Music and Technology

Music and technology are closely connected. Artists use computers and digital audio software to record, edit, mix, and distribute music. Streaming platforms also depend on data to organize music catalogs and understand how listeners interact with songs.
